# Formation-time gradient accounting (exploratory)

Question: how do individual task gradients, their cancellation, and Adam second moments develop along the existing matched formation histories?

This locally fixed descriptive analysis follows knowledge of endpoint results and review-derived hypotheses. It is exploratory, not a new confirmatory test. Use all 40 follow-up seeds 20000--20039, both directions and both routes (160 histories), the original 100-step ancestor and 500 formation steps, unchanged batches, observation schedule, loss weights, CPU float32 Adam, beta2=0.999 and weight decay=1e-5. Do not tune learning or exclude seeds by results. Technical failure stops interpretation and is retained; fixes must be documented. No duration sweep or natural-data calibration is included.

Before each original update, compute both unweighted head gradients on the actual training batch with autograd.grad. Use the unchanged original weighted loss backward and optimizer step for learning. Gradients must not alter RNG, parameters or state. Compare every resulting model parameter, m, v and step bitwise with the archived checkpoints. Retain actual-batch layer records for all 500 steps in both encoder layers and both heads. Keep selected coordinate snapshots at 0,1,10,50,100,250,500. No readout refitting is used during logging.

For each coordinate, expand the squared regularized gradient into weighted head squares, their cross term, regularization, and float32 rounding. Apply the original beta2 recursion separately to these terms, also retaining the discounted inherited ancestor v. Their sum must reconstruct actual v; compare both coordinate and layer sums. A negative cross term is cancellation. This is arithmetic accounting along the observed trajectory, not a causal intervention that removes cancellation while holding the subsequent learning path fixed. Record mean inverse scaling and realized update norms as secondary descriptions.

Primary descriptive summaries: final acquisition/update v ratio by encoder layer; fraction of accumulated weighted head-square term canceled by the cross term; relative contributions of inherited v, weighted head squares, cross term, regularization and rounding to final v. Average paired directions within seed, using logs for positive ratios. Report 5000 paired-seed bootstrap intervals, RNG 2026101702, individual rather than simultaneous 95% coverage. Keep all layers and conditions, with no outcome-dependent metric selection. Report actual-batch gradient cosine and squared norms in prespecified windows 1--100,101--250,251--500 and all 500 steps. Stratify balanced update-route observations by which target is precise as a secondary description. Plot v ratio and cancellation over time. Initial states are shared; differences later can jointly reflect inputs, weights, model geometry and their evolving interaction.
