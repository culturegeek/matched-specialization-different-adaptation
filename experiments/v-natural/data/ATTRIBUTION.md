# Source data

Luis Candanedo (2017), Appliances Energy Prediction, UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VC8G . Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/374/appliances+energy+prediction . Licensed by the source under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The original CSV is preserved unchanged; its hash and download are in source_provenance.json.

Original study: Luis M. Candanedo, Véronique Feldheim, Dominique Deramaix, Data driven prediction models of energy use of appliances in a low-energy house, Energy and Buildings 140 (2017), 81–97, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2017.01.083 .

This experiment selects the temperature/humidity pairs for kitchen, living room, and laundry room, standardizes using the training block, and adds controlled observation noise. It studies multi-output reconstruction of the recorded measurements, not the original appliance-energy benchmark and not future forecasting. The source records are measurements, not noiseless physical ground truth. The present author does not claim authorship of this external dataset.
